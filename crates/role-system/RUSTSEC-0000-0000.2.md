# RoleBuilder::deny granted instead of denying

`RoleBuilder::deny(..)` was implemented as a permission with the condition `|_| false`: a grant that never matches. A role built with `allow("x")` and `deny("x")`, with `deny` before `allow`, or with `allow("*")` plus `deny("x")` therefore still allowed `x`: the deny rule had no effect on any check. Applications that relied on `deny` to carve exceptions out of broad grants were granting access they meant to refuse.

Affected: 1.1.1, 1.2.0 and 1.2.2 (the published versions that contain `RoleBuilder::deny`; 0.1.0, 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 do not have it). Fixed in 1.3.0: a deny now overrides allows across roles.

Impact: authorization bypass for any rule expressed with `deny`. Action: upgrade to 1.3.0 and review every role built with `deny`; behaviour that was previously (wrongly) allowed is now refused.
