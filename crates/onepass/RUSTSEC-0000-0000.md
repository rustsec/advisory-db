# onepass may lose entropy for some very large schemas

When processing large repetitions (`{a}` or `{a,b}`), onepass would either panic or, in some cases, calculate passwords with less entropy than implied by the schema.

Schemas with entropy of 128 bits or less are unaffected.

The entropy lost in affected schemas is roughly proportional to the size of the expression inside the repetition; for single character classes, the total entropy lost was about 8 bits, and the total entropy in the schema was at least 247 bits.

For very large repetitions (e.g. nested groups like `([01]{256}){0,1}`) the schema could completely lose entropy from the repetition.

The flaw was corrected in onepass 3.3.0 by computing sizes of repetitions correctly.
