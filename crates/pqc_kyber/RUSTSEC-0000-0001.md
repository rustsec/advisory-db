# pqc_kyber is unmaintained

The crate has had no releases since 0.7.1 (2023-08-23), and the upstream
repository shows no maintainer activity. Open pull requests, including a fix for
a chosen-ciphertext key-recovery flaw in the AVX2 backend
(Argyle-Software/kyber#121), have gone unanswered.

Users should migrate to a maintained ML-KEM implementation, such as the
`ml-kem` crate or `libcrux-ml-kem`.
