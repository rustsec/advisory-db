# Reading an archive takes time quadratic in its entries

With the `archive` feature, `brodo::archive::from_bytes` and `brodo::archive::read`
found each entry's chunks, and its number among the entries of its kind, by scanning
every entry, so a read took time growing as the square of the archive's entry count.
The `limit` argument bounds bytes, not entries, so an archive of many small entries
holds the reading thread far beyond its size: 16,000 entries of one byte (2 MB) took
242 ms where 1,000 took 1 ms, and an archive of tens of megabytes takes minutes.

The flaw was corrected in commit 3729ea6, released as 1.3.1: a read takes time in
proportion to the entries and chunks. Before 1.3.1, an untrusted archive can be
checked with `archive::list`, which is not affected, and refused when it holds more
entries than the program needs.
