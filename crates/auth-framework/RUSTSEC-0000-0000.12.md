# Revoked access tokens still accepted by the axum and actix integration extractors

The bearer-token extractors of the web-framework integrations (axum `AuthenticatedUser` / `auth_middleware`, actix-web, warp, unified) validated the signature and claims only. They did not read the revocation marker that logout and revocation write, and they accepted refresh tokens and tokens of deactivated or deleted accounts. `AuthFramework::revoke_token` also did not write the marker the bearer paths read. Applies to applications that protect their own routes with these integrations or call `authenticate("jwt")`.

Impact: a stolen or logged-out access token stayed usable on routes protected by the integrations for the rest of its lifetime (one hour by default); a deactivated account's tokens kept working there.

Affected: 0.5.0-rc18 up to 0.6.0-rc45, including the latest 0.5.x release. 0.4.x and earlier are not affected (there is no revocation marker in the code at all). Fixed in 0.6.0-rc46; there is no patched release on 0.5.x. Users sign in again once after the upgrade. Workaround: keep access-token lifetimes short and validate tokens through `validate_api_token` rather than `TokenManager::validate_jwt_token`.
