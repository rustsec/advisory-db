# `From<DateTime<Tz>> for SystemTime` could panic outside the platform's `SystemTime` range

In 0.4.46, converting a `DateTime` to `std::time::SystemTime` with `From` or `Into` could panic when
target-specific `SystemTime` arithmetic exceeded its range. On Windows with Rust 1.94 or later, that
range extends from January 1601 to September 30828. On `wasm32-unknown-unknown`, `SystemTime` cannot
represent instants before `UNIX_EPOCH`. Applications that convert dates parsed from untrusted input
could be made to panic. 0.4.46 documented this panic, but the project's security policy treats
panics in infallible conversions as in scope.

0.4.47 and 0.5.0 normalize leap seconds before conversion, use checked epoch arithmetic, and
saturate when that arithmetic reaches its target-specific limit.
