# `TimeDelta::checked_mul` can return an out-of-range duration

For some products outside `TimeDelta`'s documented range (`TimeDelta::MIN` to `TimeDelta::MAX`),
`TimeDelta::checked_mul` returned `Some` containing an out-of-range duration instead of `None`, and
`TimeDelta * i32` returned the same value instead of panicking. Converting that duration to whole
milliseconds could panic with overflow checks enabled or wrap with them disabled.

Applications are exposed when untrusted input influences a duration or multiplier and the
application relies on the checked result for subsequent arithmetic. The observable effects include
an incorrect duration and, depending on subsequent use and overflow settings, a panic.

In 0.4.48 and 0.5.1, `checked_mul` returns `None` for an out-of-range product, and the `*` operator
reports overflow by panicking with its documented message. In-range products are unchanged.
