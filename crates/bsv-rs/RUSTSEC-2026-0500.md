# BEEF validation answers valid for a raw entry that is not a transaction

`Beef::merge_raw_tx` and `BeefTx::from_raw_tx` accept raw bytes without a
`Result`. For bytes that are not exactly one transaction (a transaction
followed by bytes, a cut transaction, bytes that are no transaction at all),
`Beef::sort_txs`, `verify_structure`, `verify_valid` and `is_valid` could
answer valid, even with no BUMP and no parent in the BEEF, and, where the entry
carried a BUMP index, treat the hash of those bytes as a proven transaction id;
an entry that names it as a parent was answered valid too.

The wire readers (`Beef::from_binary`, `verify_stream`,
`Transaction::from_beef`, `from_atomic_beef`) parse every entry and are not
affected. Reaching this needs an application that passes bytes it did not
parse to `merge_raw_tx` or `BeefTx::from_raw_tx` and then relies on the verdict
or on the transaction the BEEF hands back.

0.8.5 refuses such an entry at all four verdict doors, with or without a BUMP
index, and every entry that names it as a parent. Where upgrading is not
possible, do not rely on `is_valid` or `verify_valid` alone for such an entry:
check that the bytes are exactly one transaction before merging them.
