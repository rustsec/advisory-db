# `buffer_as_slice` builds a `&[T]` without checking `from_raw_parts`

`workflow_core::utils::buffer_as_slice` and `buffer_as_slice_mut` are safe. Each turns a `&[u8]` into `&[T]` or `&mut [T]` by adding `byte_offset` to the byte pointer, casting it to `*const T` or `*mut T`, and calling `from_raw_parts` or `from_raw_parts_mut` with the caller-supplied `elements`.

They do not check that the resulting pointer is aligned for `T`, or that `byte_offset` and `elements` stay inside `data`. Safe callers can pass an odd offset for `T = u32`, or an `elements` larger than the buffer. Either call is undefined behavior. `from_raw_parts` requires the pointer to be non-null, aligned, and the slice to point at that many initialized values.

The functions have been public since 0.1.0 and are unchanged in 0.19.0, the current crates.io release. The report has been open since 2025-09-16 with no maintainer reply and no patched release.
