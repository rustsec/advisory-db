# Malformed pack data can panic or cause excessive allocations

Malformed pack-entry and delta data can reach unchecked indexing and assertions in `gix-pack`, causing a panic. Separately, attacker-declared decompressed and delta-result sizes are used for buffer allocation before the corresponding data is validated, including preallocation of compressed-byte storage from an unrelated decompressed-size field.

A consumer that processes an untrusted pack, for example while cloning or fetching, can fail or terminate through an uncaught panic or excessive memory allocation. The exact process outcome depends on panic handling, allocator behavior, and operating-system memory policy; an immediate operating-system kill is not guaranteed.

`gix-pack` 0.69.0 fixed the initial delta-decoding issues but left the reported streaming preallocation and delta-tree buffer allocations unprotected. The follow-up changes first published in 0.73.0 remove preallocation from the declared decompressed size and apply fallible, limit-aware allocation during delta traversal. Upgrade to 0.73.0 or newer and configure an appropriate allocation limit when processing untrusted data. These changes address the described sinks; they do not make arbitrary pack processing immune to resource exhaustion.

Reported by [kodareef5](https://github.com/kodareef5).
