# `SortedLinkedList::zeroize` lead to an inconsistent state

The `Zeroize` implementation of `SortedLinkedList` did not properly re-initialize the linked list, leading to broken invariants and memorry corruption.

Reported by [Jacob Finkelman](https://github.com/Eh2406).
