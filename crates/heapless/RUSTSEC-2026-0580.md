# The treiber stack implementation in heapless had a race condition

In the affected versions, it was possible for two threads or execution contexts running concurrently to race, causing one to read out of a pointer that had been invalidated by another execution context.

Reported by [Jacob Finkelman](https://github.com/Eh2406).
