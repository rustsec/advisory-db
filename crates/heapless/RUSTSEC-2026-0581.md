# `Deque::make_contiguous` could lead to broken invariants

In some branches, `Deque::make_contiguous` set the `back` value to either `0` or `N` when it shouldn't, leading to broken invariants and out-of-bounds writes.

Reported by [DRMacIver](https://github.com/DRMacIver) and [https://github.com/MJUCOM](https://github.com/MJUCOM).
