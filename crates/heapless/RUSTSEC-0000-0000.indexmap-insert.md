# On maps of `u16::MAX` elements, `IndexMap::insert` could lead to a broken invariant

Note that this required filling up the map and having the last item inserted to hash to `u16::MAX`. It is therefore very unlikely to be reachable in a real-world scenario.

Reported by [8rsrf7](https://github.com/8rsrf7).
