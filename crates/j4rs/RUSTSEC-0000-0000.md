# `to_rust_string` and `drop_c_string` were safe public FFI helpers

`to_rust_string` was `pub fn` and called `CStr::from_ptr` on a caller-supplied `*const c_char`. `drop_c_string` was `pub fn` and called `CString::from_raw`. Safe Rust could pass a null, dangling, or non-owned pointer.

The maintainer confirmed this and, in 0.22.0 (2025-01-17), made both `pub(crate) unsafe fn`. 0.21.0, the release current when the reports were opened in November 2024, still had the safe public signatures. `to_rust_string` has been public since 0.1.0.
