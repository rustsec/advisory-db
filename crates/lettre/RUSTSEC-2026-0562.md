# Panic on an `EHLO` reply line of only white space allows denial of service

A malicious or compromised server can reply to `EHLO` with a line holding
nothing but white space, causing the client to panic.

Applications that connect only to a trusted server over implicit TLS are not
exposed; the risk applies to clients that may connect to untrusted servers, or
that use `STARTTLS` or no TLS, where a man-in-the-middle can forge the reply.
