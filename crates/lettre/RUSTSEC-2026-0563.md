# Messages for an address with a quoted local part are sent to a different address

A message whose sender or recipient has a quoted local part is sent with a
different address in its envelope than the one the application was given:
`"d "@example.com` becomes `d@example.com`. Someone who can choose such an
address can have a message delivered to another mailbox than the one the
application validated.

Addresses without a quoted local part are not affected.
