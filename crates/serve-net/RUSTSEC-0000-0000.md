# Untrusted client can set the authority seen in `call` when `trust_host` is on

On a listener behind forwarded headers with `ProxySource::trust_host(true)`, a client that was not a
trusted proxy could send `X-Forwarded-Host` or `Forwarded: host=` and have that value reported by
`HttpHead::authority()` inside `HttpService::call`. The authority seen in `head` was correct; the view
of the request built again for `call` read the forwarded host without checking that the client had
been resolved through a trusted hop.

A service that routes virtual hosts, builds absolute links or redirects, or keys a cache by the
authority inside `call` could be steered by any client that reached the listener directly.

Listeners with `trust_host` off (the default), direct listeners, and PROXY protocol listeners are not
affected, and neither are the client address and the scheme.

The issue is fixed in 0.6.2, and versions 0.3.0 to 0.6.1 are yanked.
